We keep job applications for up to 12 months after receipt unless agreed otherwise. Business enquiries are kept for the duration of the business communication and then for up to 3 years. If a contract is concluded, we keep data for the periods required by law (e.g. accounting).

We do not sell data. Only the people at XO-SERVICES who handle your application or enquiry and our e-mail provider (Websupport, s. r. o., Slovakia) process it. For an application to a specific project we may, with your consent, pass your details to the client you would work for. Our external accounting firm receives data only once a contract exists.

Fonts and images are served from our own website. The site is hosted on GitHub Pages (GitHub, Inc., USA), which technically processes your IP address when serving the page to keep the service running; see GitHub's privacy statement for details.
