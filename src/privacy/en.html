No, there is no legal obligation. Without your name, contact details and profession, however, we cannot handle your application or enquiry. Phone number and CV are optional.

We keep job applications for up to 12 months after receipt unless agreed otherwise. Business enquiries are kept for the duration of the business communication and then for up to 3 years. If a contract is concluded, we keep data for the periods required by law (e.g. accounting).
